\section{Objectives} 
\label{chap:Objectives}

During the academic year of the Master's in Electronic Systems, in the Embedded 
Systems course, several laboratory exercises were conducted using the STM32F4-
DISCOVERY board. These exercises typically employed the I²C communication 
protocol. The main objective of this thesis is to contribute to the development of 
the \textbf{MICROLAB} and \textbf{SIVALSE} projects by incorporating the I²C bus into 
the STM32F4 microcontroller and accelerometer peripheral.

To achieve this main goal, the following \textbf{specific objectives} have been defined:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item\textbf{Analyze and understand} the architecture of the STM32F405 microcontroller 
        and the LIS3DH accelerometer, focusing on their communication through 
        the Inter-Integrated Circuit (I$^2$C) protocol.\\\\
    \textit{This theoretical and technical understanding provides the foundation for 
        accurate emulation of real hardware behavior.}

    \item \textbf{Develop an emulation model of the STM32F405 I$^2$C controller} within 
        the QEMU virtualization framework.\\\\
    \textit{The model must implement register-level behavior, protocol timing, and 
        interrupt generation to ensure compatibility with embedded firmware.}

    \item \textbf{Implement a virtual model of the LIS3DH accelerometer} as an I$^2$C 
        slave device in QEMU.\\\\
    \textit{This model will reproduce the sensor's registers, operating modes, and 
        three-axis acceleration data output, allowing the emulated STM32F405 to interact 
        with it as if it were a physical device.}

    \item \textbf{Integrate both models into a unified virtual system} capable of executing 
        real STM32 firmware within QEMU.\\\\
    \textit{The integration must simulate the interaction between the microcontroller and 
        the accelerometer accurately, supporting complete I$^2$C data exchanges.}

    \item \textbf{Validate the emulated system using STM32 HAL} \\\\
    \textit{Firmware developed with STM32CubeIDE will be executed on the 
        virtual platforms to confirm the functionality with the HAL.}

    \item \textbf{Document and evaluate} the system's performance, limitations, and 
        educational potential within the MICROLAB environment.\\\\
    \textit{The results will serve as a reference for future integration of additional 
        peripherals and tools to enhance virtual experimentation.}
\end{enumerate}
