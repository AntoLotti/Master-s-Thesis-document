\section{Motivation}
\label{chap:Motivation}

Often, both individuals and institutions face budgetary constraints when making 
decisions. Universities are no exception to this problem; they frequently operate 
under severe budgetary limitations, resulting in outdated or insufficient laboratory 
equipment, which directly compromises the quality of practical instruction in critical 
engineering disciplines.

An example of this situation can be found at any public university. These 
institutions have hundreds of students enrolled in some branch of engineering. 
Many, if not all, engineering programs include laboratory work, which typically requires 
access to specialized hardware. An example of this type of hardware could be a 
development board like the STM32F405 Discovery.

This hardware is usually provided to students by the faculty. 
However, due to the large number of students, it is not feasible for the university to provide, 
even temporarily, one unit of this equipment per student, thus limiting student access to 
the hardware only during laboratory sessions. This limitation forces students to 
perform laboratory exercises in pairs and to rotate between different groups during practical exams. 
Furthermore, the scarcity of equipment prevents students from 
accessing it outside of scheduled laboratory sessions, thus limiting their opportunities 
for independent experimentation.
  
In the field of embedded systems, a possible solution to the problems raised in 
the preceding paragraphs could be the use of software-based hardware emulation. 
A virtual environment that emulates the behavior of real hardware would allow 
students, or anyone with the goal of learning, to work with realistic microcontroller 
firmware, registers, and I²C peripherals, thus avoiding typical hardware problems 
such as damaged boards, wiring errors, or missing components.

This is the motivation of the project: to show how the use of software-based 
hardware emulation can be a useful tool in teaching embedded software when physical 
hardware is required but not available.
