\subsection{I2C Terminology}
\label{chap:Back_I2C_Terminology}

In the table \ref{tab:i2c_terminology} there are some important terms/concepts related with the 
I2C protocol, that will be needed before entering in deep how the I2C works.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{|l | l|}
        \hline
        TERM            & DESCRIPTION \\        
        \hline
        Transmitter     & The device which sends data to the bus \\
        \hline
        Receiver        & The device which receives data from the bus\\
        \hline
        Master          & The device which initiates a transfer, generates\\ 
                        & clock signals and terminates a transfer \\
        \hline
        Slave           & The device addressed by a master \\
        \hline
        Multi-master    & More than one master can attempt to control the \\
                        &  bus at the same time without corrupting the message \\
        \hline
        Arbitration     & Procedure to ensure that, if more than one master \\
                        & simultaneously tries to control the bus, only one \\ 
                        & is allowed to do so and the winning message is not \\
                        & corrupted \\
        \hline
        Synchronization & Procedure to synchronize the clock signals of two \\
                        & or more devices \\
        \hline
    \end{tabular}

    \caption
        {
            Definition of I$^2$C-bus terminology \cite{I2CSpec2000}
        }
    \label{tab:i2c_terminology}
\end{table}
